# Email Troubleshooting Guide

## Symptoms

Users report missing email, Outlook sync failures, calendar delays, or repeated authentication prompts.

## First Checks

Check the email service status. Confirm whether mail flow, calendar sync, and identity login are operational.

## User Steps

Ask the user to restart the mail client, verify network connectivity, check mailbox quota, and try webmail. If webmail works but the desktop client fails, collect client logs and device details.

## Escalation

Escalate to Collaboration when multiple users are affected, mail flow is delayed, or calendar sync remains broken after client restart.
