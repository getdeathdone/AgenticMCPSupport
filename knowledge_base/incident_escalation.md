# Incident Escalation Guide

## Severity

Use SEV2 for major degradation affecting many users or business-critical workflows. Use SEV3 for limited degradation with available workarounds.

## Communication

Tell users the current incident status, affected service, known workaround, and next update timing. Avoid promising an exact resolution time unless the incident owner has published one.

## Escalation

Escalate to the owning team when multiple users are affected, monitoring shows service degradation, or support tickets cluster around the same symptom.
